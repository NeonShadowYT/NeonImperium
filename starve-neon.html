Description

⚔️ WOKE UP IN THE RUINS

No memory, no gear, no allies.

First stone, first axe, first step into the forest. Come back alive - become stronger. Die - lose everything.

There is no grind for the sake of grind. Progression is tied to risk: the more dangerous the raid - the greater the reward.

☠️ DEATH - TUTORIAL

Took gear into the raid - be ready to lose it.

Like in Tarkov: you die - your loot goes to the enemy. No 'save before battle'. No second chances. Just you and your skill.

🔥 RISK-BASED PROGRESSION

The tutorial holds your hand, but after 10 minutes you decide: run for gold through bears or return to base.

Want better gear? Go into the hottest spot. Forest, mountain, volcano. Each biome is a step up in difficulty.

🧬 YOUR BUILD

Every buff comes with a debuff. No OP without consequences.

Genetics is a balance game. Balance metabolism like RimWorld, unlocking genes for your playstyle.

🌍 THE WORLD DOESN'T WAIT

Each biome has its own temperature, resources, creatures. Without bear fur you'll freeze in the mountain. Without ice you'll burn in the volcano.

The AI storyteller watches you. It loves surprises: faction caravans, pack hunts, rare plants. It always knows where you are. And always ready to make your day more interesting.

🏰 YOUR ISLAND - YOUR FORTRESS

Build walls, set up workbenches, reinforce the foundation. Like in Rust.

But without neighbors who come at night while you're offline. Each player has their own private island.

Factions can raid - and they're more dangerous than any player. Plans: clan bases and raids on other islands. For now - defend and build.

🤝 FACTIONS

Crab-people pirates, werewolves in bone caves, ancient guardians of the desert, samurai by the volcano. You can trade, befriend, or fight them.

Each faction has its own currency, resources, components. Want the best gun? Earn reputation through quests, arena battles. Or take it by force.

⚡ PLAY YOUR WAY

PC and phone - in the same lobby. No FPS discounts. No donations or ads. Everything for gameplay. Season pass without deadlines - play at your own pace.

Log in daily - get currency for skins. Craft on the go, auto-equip, auto-eat, auto-run, auto-jump.

I made the game convenient, but not easy.

📋 QUICK SUMMARY

☠️ Death in raid = loss of all gear
⚡ Fast progression through risk, not hours of grind
🏝️ Each player has own island - no offline raids
🧬 Genetics: Fit your playstyle, weaken the rest
👁️ Living world with a storyteller that never sleeps
🤝 Factions with reputation and unique resources
🎄 Seasonal events, wipes, mod support and editor even on mobile
📱 Cross-platform, free, no donations and no ads
